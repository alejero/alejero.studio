About

I never learned to pick just one.

alejerostudio is the name I put on everything I make: photographs, drawings, identities, websites. It's one person rather than a team, and that's deliberate. The same judgment that decides where to stand for a photo decides where the type goes and how fast the page loads.

Most of what's here I made because I wanted to. The rest was for people who asked. Both get the same attention.
